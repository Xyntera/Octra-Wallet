Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Non-Custodial Nature

Octra Wallet is a non-custodial application. We do not have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or sensitive financial data. Cryptographic keys are stored locally on your device using platform secure storage where available.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. Octra Wallet does not require registration, email addresses, or phone numbers to operate. Your transaction data is public on the Octra blockchain but is handled using Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) for maximum privacy.

3. Third-Party Services

The application interacts with the Octra Network via RPC nodes. These nodes may see your public wallet address and transaction metadata, but they cannot access your private keys or decrypt your FHE-protected balances.

4. Security

You are solely responsible for the security of your device and the backup of your seed phrase. Loss of your seed phrase will result in the permanent loss of your OCT tokens.

5. Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at octrawallet.app/privacy.html.
