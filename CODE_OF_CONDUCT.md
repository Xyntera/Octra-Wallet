# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussions technical, respectful, and
constructive.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- publishing someone else's private information
- spam or repeated off-topic comments
- attempts to trick maintainers or users into revealing secrets

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or reject
contributions that violate this standard.

Security-sensitive discussions should follow `SECURITY.md`.
